# RResizer

Resize and convert photos and videos on your Mac: JPEG, HEIC, PNG, TIFF, WebP, GIF and DNG, a size limit in MB, crop, black & white looks, film borders and grain, PDF pages to images, and video in H.264, HEVC or ProRes with LUTs. Your originals are never changed.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the newest version](https://github.com/robertarnar98/RResizer-Download/releases/latest)**: click **RResizer.dmg** under *Assets*.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click **RResizer.dmg**.
2. Drag **RResizer** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. That's it: RResizer is signed and approved by Apple, so it opens with no security warning.

## Updates

You don't need to come back here. When there's a new version, a card pops up in the middle of RResizer. Click **Update Now** and it updates itself in a few seconds and reopens. You can also choose **RResizer ▸ Check for Updates…** any time.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or newer · Apple Silicon and Intel Macs · by Tungl Media
